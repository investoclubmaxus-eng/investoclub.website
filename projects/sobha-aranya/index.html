The sectors between NH-48 and the Aravallis, where the city is still forming.

New Gurugram covers the sectors that extend south and west of the older city — broadly the 76 to 95 range along with the belt served directly by NH-48. It holds a wide spread of product, from ultra-luxury schemes backing onto the Aravalli ridge to industrial and warehousing development along the highway, and it is where the largest single land parcels in the city remain available.

The developer
