Open larger map

The corridor that carries Gurugram’s luxury address southward.

Golf Course Extension Road runs south from the original Golf Course Road, and over the last decade it has absorbed most of the city’s new luxury supply. The corridor is wide, largely grade-separated, and lined with a mix of high-rise residential, office and retail — which is why it now reads as a continuation of the established Golf Course Road rather than a peripheral sector.
