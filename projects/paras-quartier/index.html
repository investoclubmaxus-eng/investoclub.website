Location

Sector 2, Gurugram · Golf Course Road, Gurugram

The city’s original luxury address, and still its most established.

Golf Course Road is the corridor Gurugram’s luxury market was built on. It runs through the older, fully formed part of the city, where the schools, hospitals, retail and offices are already in place rather than promised — which is why land here trades at a premium to every newer corridor and why the schemes built on it tend to be small and very high-value.
