The link that lets the southern sectors bypass the older centre.

The Southern Peripheral Road connects Golf Course Extension Road to NH-48 across the south of the city, and it has become the reason a large share of recent premium launches sit where they do. It allows movement between the luxury corridor and the highway without passing through the older centre, which materially changes drive times from the sectors it serves.
