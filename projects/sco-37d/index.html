Location

Sector 37D, Gurugram · Dwarka Expressway, Gurugram

The newest arterial corridor, and the city’s fastest-changing address.

The Dwarka Expressway links Gurugram to Dwarka in south-west Delhi, and its completion reorganised how the northern sectors of the city are reached. Sectors along its length — 37D, 79, 112, 113 and their neighbours — hold a large share of Gurugram’s current residential and commercial pipeline, alongside the Yashobhoomi convention centre and Diplomatic Enclave II on the Delhi side.
